Designed for your workflow

A focused Windows dictation tool with local-first transcription and flexible text polishing.

Dictate anywhere

Use a configurable global hotkey to start dictating into the application you’re working in, with a lightweight floating status pill.

Local transcription

Speech recognition runs on your PC using a local Whisper model. If you choose cloud text polishing, that transcript is sent to the selected provider.

Your words, your terms

Add names and specialist vocabulary, choose writing prompts, and review saved dictations in the searchable local Vault.
