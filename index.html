VeeType floats silently in the background. Press your custom hotkey and a glassmorphic pill appears, ready to listen across any Windows app.

2. WebRTC Noise Suppression.

Mechanical keyboard clicks? Fan noise? Gone. Our Voice Activity Detector (VAD) strips ambient audio instantly, feeding pristine vocals to the engine.

VeeType dynamically detects your hardware. Got an NVIDIA GPU? It routes via CUDA for instantaneous output. Standard laptop? It falls back to optimized CPU threads automatically.

The local LLM polishes your phrasing, drops the filler words, injects your custom jargon, and pastes the result seamlessly into your IDE, browser, or Word doc.
